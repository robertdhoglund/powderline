# Playtest checklist (v0.2)

Automated: `npm run check` and `npm test`. Everything below needs a person and a real desktop browser.

**Loads and runs**

- [ ] Title screen appears with no console errors. DROP IN starts the run.
- [ ] Frame rate feels smooth. Note where it drops.
- [ ] `P` pauses and resumes. `R` restarts instantly. The finish screen shows a rank and RIDE AGAIN works.

**Ride**

- [ ] `A` / `D` carve; the board leans and throws snow off the outside edge.
- [ ] Releasing a held carve gives a small forward pump.
- [ ] `W` is faster, `S` brakes with a big spray.
- [ ] Hands off the keys, the board follows the piste.
- [ ] Steep pitches feel steep. The icefall jump is a clear big-air moment.
- [ ] Landings thump: snow burst, camera dip.
- [ ] Hitting a tree costs speed and flow, and you can carry on.

**Bow**

- [ ] Holding the mouse slows time and pulls the camera in. Slow motion is stronger in the air.
- [ ] The arrow lands where the reticle pointed, at short and long range.
- [ ] Hits pop with a freeze-frame; core hits say PERFECT.
- [ ] Right click cancels a draw. Holding too long empties focus and the aim sways.
- [ ] You can steer and shoot in the same breath without it feeling unfair.

**Ice**

- [ ] Riding onto a sheet: cracks race ahead and the sheet breaks up around you.
- [ ] Shooting a wall: cracks spread from the arrow and the wall blows away from you.
- [ ] Riding through a wall says CRASH and slows you, and you can carry on.

**Tuning questions**

Too twitchy, too slow, or right? Does the spray look like powder or like fog? Is aiming fair? Which section is the most fun, and which drags? After how many runs do you stop wanting another?
