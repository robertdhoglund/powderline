# First playtest checklist

Check a **real desktop browser** before calling this version fully functional.

- [ ] The page loads without console errors and the Start button appears.
- [ ] Clicking Start removes the opening screen, shows the rider/slope and starts motion.
- [ ] Trees and snowy ground are visible; no black screen or inverted terrain.
- [ ] A and D carve in opposite directions; stopping input eases side motion.
- [ ] W makes you faster and S noticeably brakes.
- [ ] Board leaves two thin indented-looking snow grooves; carve makes a side spray.
- [ ] Fine snow stringers trail behind the board. Snow landing emits a burst.
- [ ] Space makes you jump and you land smoothly back onto the snow.
- [ ] Mouse reticle moves correctly; hold left mouse to draw, release to fire.
- [ ] A target under the crosshair can be hit by an arrow and disappears with score.
- [ ] Ice fractures when riding onto it; arrow impact can also break ice.
- [ ] Shards scatter and eventually disappear, with no major frame rate drop.
- [ ] P pauses and resumes. R restarts cleanly.
- [ ] Run ends after 900 meters and offers a restart.
- [ ] Sound works after clicking Start; no unexpected music or audio samples.

**Tuning questions:** Are turns too snappy, too floaty, or right? Are powder puffs visibly soft and stringy? Does the snowboard look like it's *inside* the snow rather than hovering over it? Is archery fair while carving? How many seconds before you want to restart voluntarily?
